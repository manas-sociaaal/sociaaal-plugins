# Cappuccino — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
Audio social app for keeping in touch with friends. Daily voice notes with your group — short audio updates, like a podcast for your friend group. Audio-social category. Low AI-fit (real friends are the content).

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Close-friend groups, long-distance friendships, Gen Z + young millennials (18–28) who hate texting and don't want to FaceTime.
- **Secondary:** College friends post-graduation, expat communities, parents away from kids.
- **Where they live:** Where the friend group already lives (iMessage, WhatsApp groups). TikTok #voicenotes / #longdistancefriends content. Reddit r/friendship.
- **Motivation:** Stay close to friends without scheduling a call. Hear their voice. Daily ambient connection.

## Core promise
**Stay close with the friends you don't see daily — through their voice, not their text.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Daily voice "newspaper" from your friend group
2. Async — no scheduling needed
3. Voice = warmer than text, faster than a call
4. Bonds over time — shared audio archive

## Format playbook
**Works:** audio-led storytelling with screen recording, split-screen friend reactions, POV listening to a voice note, talking-head "this is how I keep in touch" testimonial.
**Doesn't:** fast-cut content that loses the audio thread, AI-heavy visuals (real friend warmth is the product).
**AI-asset fit:** Light. Most footage is real friend interactions or stock-style B-roll. AI used for animated waveforms, vibey connecting B-roll, transition graphics.

## Competitor context
Differentiate from Voxer (older walkie-talkie app, less polished), Marco Polo (video-first), iMessage voice notes (no curation, mixed in with text). Cappuccino's lane is the **dedicated app that turns friend voice notes into a daily product**.

## Don't-do list
- Don't scripted-read "real" friend conversations — they read fake.
- Avoid overly polished / aspirational framings. The product's truth is intimacy + casualness.
- Don't make it look like Snapchat's reverse — different value prop.

## Open research questions
- Solo creator demos vs friend-pair demos — which converts better?
- Long-distance specific framing (expat, military, college spread) — is that the wedge?
- Daily-streak hooks — does gamification angle help?
