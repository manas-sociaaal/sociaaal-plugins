# Toned — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
AI skin tone / undertone analyzer. Photo-based color season analysis for makeup and fashion. Beauty-analysis category. High-AI-fit product — palette overlays and before/after colors are the visual identity.

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Women 18–35 who are color-curious, makeup buyers, fashion-aware, doing color-analysis content themselves.
- **Secondary:** Wedding shoppers (bridesmaid-color decisions), capsule-wardrobe planners, image consultants.
- **Where they live:** TikTok #colorseason / #colorAnalysis / #undertonetest (HUGE niche, billions of cumulative views), Pinterest color-palette boards, Instagram fashion creators.
- **Motivation:** Buy the right makeup the first time. Wear colors that "actually work." Decode why some outfits look amazing and others don't.

## Core promise
**Know your real undertone and color season — buy the right makeup, wear the right colors.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Photo-based — no in-person consultant needed (saves $200–500 per analysis)
2. Personalized color palette output — shareable and shoppable
3. Before/after wrong-vs-right color overlays
4. Reuse for outfit decisions, makeup shopping, hair color

## Past creative observations
- P0_X folder in `NEW PHASE GATE/TONED/`. (Review on next run.)
- Likely formats: selfie-to-palette reveal, "wrong colors → right colors" outfit comparisons, undertone-test demos.

## Cultural moments to ride
- The color-analysis trend is currently MASSIVE on TikTok (billions of views, sustained for 2+ years)
- Seasonal fashion content cycles (spring/summer color drops drive curiosity)
- Wedding season (bridesmaid color decisions)
- Capsule wardrobe trends
- "I did color analysis and…" creator testimonials

## Format playbook
**Works:** selfie-to-palette reveal, before/after color overlay, outfit comparison with the "right colors", screen recording of analysis flow.
**Doesn't:** voice-driven content (this is visual-first), male-targeted framings (audience is overwhelmingly women).
**AI-asset fit:** Strong. Palette generation, color swatches, before/after color overlays — all AI-handled. NanoBanana for the seasonal portrait stylings.

## Competitor context
Differentiate from professional color analysts (expensive, in-person), House of Color books (analog, dated), generic skin-tone matching apps. Toned's lane is **fast, photo-based, comprehensive color-season output**.

## Don't-do list
- **Color analysis is debated** — frame as "AI analysis" not as a definitive scientific result. Avoid medical/clinical framing.
- Don't only show light-skinned users — the system must demonstrate inclusivity across the full skin tone range. Critical for trust.
- Avoid promising "guaranteed accuracy" — frame as a tool, not a verdict.
- Avoid pricey-cosmetic-product placements that read as paid ads.
