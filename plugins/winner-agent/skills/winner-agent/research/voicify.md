# Voicify — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
AI voice changer + celebrity voice clone. Record audio → output sounds like the chosen voice (yours, your dog's, a celebrity's). Voice-AI category. Audio-led product — visuals support, sound is the punchline.

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Comedy / parody creators, kids and teens (13–22), prankster users, content creators looking for bits.
- **Secondary:** Older millennials using celebrity narration for comedic content, gamers, podcast tinkerers.
- **Where they live:** TikTok #aivoice / #voicechanger / #morganfreemanai, YouTube Shorts comedy bits, Twitch streamer clips.
- **Motivation:** Make funny content fast. Make a celebrity narrate something mundane. Prank a friend.

## Core promise
**Make any voice into any other voice in seconds.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Comedic value — celebrity-narrating-mundane-thing is reliably funny
2. Speed — record then output in under 30 seconds
3. Variety — large library of celeb / character voices
4. Shareable — clips work as audio in any messaging app

## Past creative observations
- P0_X folder exists in `NEW PHASE GATE/VOICIFY/`. (Pull and review on next run.)
- Likely formats: pet-voiceover gags, celebrity-narrating-life-events, "I sound like [celeb]" before/after audio reveals.
- Be aware: visuals can repeat easily because the punchline is the sound.

## Cultural moments to ride
- Trending celebrity voices in news/film (e.g. Morgan Freeman, David Attenborough — evergreen winners)
- Specific viral audio moments (interview soundbites, podcast clips)
- Celebrity narration parody trends (e.g. "Attenborough narrates my dating life")
- Pet-voiceover trends — perpetually viral

## Format playbook
**Works:** before/after audio reveal, voiceover on existing footage, screen-recording app demo with audio output, talking-head demo.
**Doesn't:** pure visual storytelling (this is an audio app — the visual must serve the sound).
**AI-asset fit:** Mixed. Visuals can be AI-generated (B-roll for the demo, mock chat screens), but the punchline is auditory. The editor will dub the final audio. AI assets primarily for visual context, not the hero moment.

## Competitor context
Differentiate from ElevenLabs (developer tool, not consumer), Resemble (also dev), Voicemod (gamer-focused real-time changer). Voicify's lane is **fast consumer voice transformation for content** — short clips, share-ready, broad voice library.

## Don't-do list
- **Never use a celebrity's voice in a defamatory or misleading context** — implying they said something they didn't can trigger right-of-publicity claims.
- Never voice-clone a private individual without consent.
- Avoid scammy "this is how to fool your bank" framings.
- Be cautious with political figures.
