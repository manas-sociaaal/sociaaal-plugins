# Dream Wise AI — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
AI dream interpretation. Describe a dream, get an analysis with symbolism, emotional themes, and personal-context interpretation. Spiritual-AI category. Excellent AI-fit — surreal dreamscape imagery is the visual identity.

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Spiritually-curious users (mostly women 18–40), Gen Z + millennials interested in psychology / manifestation / lucid dreaming, mystical-content consumers.
- **Secondary:** Therapy-curious folks who can't afford therapy, journal-keepers, witchy-aesthetic culture.
- **Where they live:** TikTok #spiritualtok / #manifesting / #lucidDreaming / #dreammeaning, Pinterest mystical aesthetic boards, Instagram tarot creators.
- **Motivation:** Decode what their dreams mean. Self-reflection. Manifestation tools. Curiosity about symbolism.

## Core promise
**Decode what your dreams actually mean.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Personalized interpretation (dream + your life context)
2. Symbolism library (ancient + modern + Jungian)
3. Recurring-dream tracking
4. Beautiful, mystical UI that feels like an actual dream-journal

## Format playbook
**Works:** mystical visual storytelling with voiceover, voiceover over dreamy AI imagery, screen recording of analysis output, "decode this dream" interactive series.
**Doesn't:** fast/funny content (audience is contemplative), bro-coded humor, AI-skepticism humor.
**AI-asset fit:** Excellent. Surreal dreamscapes via NanoBanana — water, doors, falling, teeth, mirrors. Slow Kling motion is on-brand. The mystical visual is half the product.

## Competitor context
Differentiate from generic dream-meaning websites (DIY dictionaries), Co-Star (astrology-focused), The Pattern (broader spiritual). Dream Wise's lane is **personalized dream interpretation with AI-quality symbolism analysis**.

## Don't-do list
- **Never frame dream meanings as medical/psychological diagnosis.** Frame as reflection / symbolism / contemplation tools.
- Avoid mocking spiritual practices — the audience takes this seriously.
- Don't promise lucid dreaming or specific outcomes.
- Avoid overly Western Christian framings — audience is more nondenominationally spiritual.
