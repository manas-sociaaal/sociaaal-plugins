# Holy Guides — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
Bible verses + devotionals. Daily Christian content tailored to the user. Religious category. Strong AI-fit — peaceful AI nature/light imagery is the brand identity.

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Christian users (US, UK, parts of Latin America), family-oriented (women 25–55 lean), faith-curious teens.
- **Secondary:** Returning-to-faith adults, parents teaching kids, daily-devotional readers.
- **Where they live:** YouVersion-adjacent communities, Christian Instagram (#christiantok, #faithcontent), TikTok #dailyverse / #scriptureoftheday, Pinterest devotional boards.
- **Motivation:** Daily faith reflection. Verse-of-the-day for journaling. Kid-friendly devotionals. Meaningful moments amid the day.

## Core promise
**Daily faith content tailored to you.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Personalized verse/devotional based on mood or topic
2. Beautiful imagery to share
3. Reading streaks + reflection prompts
4. Multi-translation support

## Past creative observations
- HOLY GUIDES folder exists in `NEW PHASE GATE/HOLY GUIDES/` (visible in Drive).
- Likely formats: text-on-image verses, voiceover devotional, calming nature B-roll with scripture overlay.

## Cultural moments to ride
- Faith-based content is a large under-served niche on TikTok (high engagement, low competition vs general AI apps)
- Easter / Christmas / Lent / Advent seasons drive seasonal spikes
- "When you need hope" / "verse for tough times" perennial formats
- Specific Bible-quoting moments tied to current events (with care)

## Format playbook
**Works:** text-on-image verses, voice-over devotional, calming nature B-roll, "when you need [emotional state]" verse-card series.
**Doesn't:** humor or irreverent tone, edgy hooks, anything that mocks faith adjacent practices.
**AI-asset fit:** Strong. Peaceful AI imagery (sunrise, nature, light, mountains, hands in prayer). Slow zoom motion is on-brand.

## Competitor context
Differentiate from YouVersion (massive incumbent, free, the standard), Bible.com, generic devotional apps. Holy Guides' lane is **personalized + beautiful daily content with AI-imagery polish that YouVersion doesn't match**.

## Don't-do list
- **Never use AI to generate religious figures' faces** (Jesus, saints, etc.) — sensitive territory both legally and culturally.
- Don't take political stances. Stay focused on personal faith content.
- Avoid prosperity-gospel tropes ("God will bless you with money").
- Don't generate culturally-imitating content for non-Christian faith traditions (this app is specifically Christian).
