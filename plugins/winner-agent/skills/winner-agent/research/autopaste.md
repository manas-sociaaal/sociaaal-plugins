# AutoPaste — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
iOS keyboard with saved phrases / auto-fill. Paste full templates with one tap. Keyboard-productivity category. Utility-first product — sells via a hack/POV demonstration.

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Working professionals (25–45) — customer service, sales, support, recruiters who type repeated messages.
- **Secondary:** Students writing similar emails, real estate agents, freelancers, anyone with templated outreach.
- **Where they live:** TikTok #productivityhack / #iphonehack / #iphonetips, LinkedIn productivity-tip creators, Reddit r/productivity, YouTube "iPhone tips & tricks" channels.
- **Motivation:** Save typing time. Stop retyping the same email/address/intro/customer reply.

## Core promise
**Stop retyping the same emails, addresses, and replies — paste full templates with a tap.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Time saved on repetitive typing (the killer metric — quantify it!)
2. Works across ALL apps (Mail, Slack, iMessage, Gmail web)
3. One-tap paste from a custom keyboard
4. Sync across devices

## Past creative observations
- P0_X folder in `NEW PHASE GATE/AUTOPASTE/`. (Review on next run.)
- Likely formats: stopwatch comparisons (slow type vs one-tap), POV "saving 2 hours a day" narratives, talking-head productivity tips.

## Cultural moments to ride
- Back-to-work / new-quarter productivity content seasons (Sept, Jan)
- "iPhone tips you didn't know" account formats — perpetually viral
- Tax season for accountants, customer-service week, hiring season for recruiters
- Productivity-influencer content cycles ("I save 10 hours a week with this trick")

## Format playbook
**Works:** screen recording of typing → paste reveal, stopwatch comparison (slow vs fast), talking-head productivity tip format, POV "I just discovered this hack."
**Doesn't:** pure entertainment / fluff — audience comes for utility, not vibes.
**AI-asset fit:** Light. Screen recordings dominate. AI used for stopwatch/clock motion graphics, B-roll of typing, productivity-vibe environment shots.

## Competitor context
Differentiate from Apple's built-in Text Replacement (limited, no key on keyboard), TextExpander (Mac/PC focused, expensive), aText (Mac). AutoPaste's lane is **dedicated iPhone keyboard with one-tap paste from a saved-phrase library**.

## Don't-do list
- Don't fabricate time-savings numbers — use realistic numbers ("save 15 minutes a day," not "save 4 hours a day").
- Don't show typing of fake personal data (don't actually screen-record SSNs, etc.).
- Avoid making it sound like a "scam tool" — it's productivity, not exploitation.
