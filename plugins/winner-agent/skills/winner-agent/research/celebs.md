# Celebs — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
AI celebrity lookalike finder. Upload a selfie → app shows which celebrity you most resemble. Photo-AI category. Identity-discovery hook with built-in social shareability.

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Identity-curious Gen Z (18–25), social-app heavy users, fan communities (especially K-pop, hip-hop, Gen Z TV).
- **Secondary:** Late millennials nostalgic for celebrity culture, friend groups doing comparison challenges.
- **Where they live:** TikTok #celebslookalike / #lookalikechallenge, Instagram tag-a-friend reaction posts, BeReal / Snapchat shares.
- **Motivation:** Validation ("am I attractive?" via celebrity comparison), social currency, conversation in friend group chats.

## Core promise
**Discover (and share) which celebrity you look like.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Validation through celebrity comparison
2. Shareable result — auto-formatted side-by-side
3. Speed — instant reveal from one selfie
4. Re-playable — change angle, get different match (drives multi-day retention)

## Past creative observations
- P0_1 through P0_10 in `NEW PHASE GATE/CELEBS/` are HEAVILY rotated on **talking-head selfie + iMessage-style yellow caption hooks** (e.g. "if your crush", "you're cooked if you haven't done it yet"). Each P0_X has 5 concepts (C1–C5), each with Base_Notexthook.mp4 + Example_V1.mp4 (text variant).
- Hook format burned: Gen Z guy POV with yellow text overlay + "if your crush" framing.
- Formats UNDERUSED that the spec lists as strengths: pure selfie-to-celeb morph reveal, side-by-side comparisons, trending-audio-driven reveals.

## Cultural moments to ride
- Currently-trending celebrities (movies/music releases — e.g. recent: #michaeljackson hit 159M views during the biopic moment)
- Award show seasons (Oscars, Grammys) — fan-cast lookalike content peaks
- Pop star tour cycles (Taylor / Sabrina / Olivia drop new aesthetic eras)
- Twin-reveal trends (siblings, cousins, "long-lost twin" formats)

## Format playbook
**Works:** selfie-to-celeb reveal, side-by-side comparison, trending-audio over reveal, decade-themed era match, comment-bait "drop a celeb name" POVs.
**Doesn't:** multi-character live action, dialogue-heavy scripts.
**AI-asset fit:** Strong. NanoBanana for celebrity-vibe stills, Kling for simple morph/match-cut motion. Editor handles the side-by-side compositing.

## Competitor context
Differentiate from Gradient (broader filters), Reface (face-swap into existing celebrity videos), FaceApp (age/gender filters). Celebs is purely lookalike-discovery.

## Don't-do list
- **Never deepfake the actual celebrity's face** — use stylized lookalikes that capture vibe/aesthetic, not facial features. Verify with reverse image search before posting.
- Don't claim accuracy. Frame as "AI thinks you look like…"
- Avoid pairing users with minors or with deceased celebrities in non-respectful framings.
- Avoid sexualized celebrity contexts.

## Open research questions
- Does the talking-head + iMessage caption format still win, or is fatigue setting in after 10 P0_X cycles?
- Side-by-side vs morph reveal — which pulls higher install rate?
- What's the optimal "celebrity" to feature? Most-trending now vs evergreen icons?
