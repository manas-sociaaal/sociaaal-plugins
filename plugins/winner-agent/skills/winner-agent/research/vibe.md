# Vibe — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
Find Snapchat friends. Social discovery for active Snap users. Social-discovery category. Light AI-fit (most footage is screen UI).

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Active Snapchat users (13–22), social-extroverted teens, lonely-at-college population, FOMO-prone Gen Z.
- **Secondary:** People who recently moved cities, friend-group expanders, casual gamers using Snap streaks.
- **Where they live:** Snapchat itself, TikTok #snapchat / #snapfriends, Reddit r/snapchat, gaming Discord servers.
- **Motivation:** More streak partners. New people to chat with. Find friends-of-friends. Fight social loneliness.

## Core promise
**Find new friends to Snap — discover people you'd actually want to know.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Snap-active user matching (not Tinder for Snap, but social discovery)
2. Curated by interest / vibe filters
3. Privacy-respecting — public Snap usernames only
4. Fast onboarding to friending

## Cultural moments to ride
- Back-to-school season (people moving to college, fresh friend networks needed)
- Snapchat streak culture moments (when streaks break, viral reactions)
- "Lonely on the internet" content (a perennial Gen Z TikTok genre)
- Pop culture Snapchat moments (when celebs add fans, etc.)

## Format playbook
**Works:** screen-recording app demo, POV first-snap reveal, friend-count glow-up, talking-head "I found my best friend on this app" testimonials.
**Doesn't:** content that needs deep narrative or long arc — Vibe is fast-action discovery.
**AI-asset fit:** Light. Most is screen UI. AI for vibe shots (lonely-bedroom, phone-glow, friend-group celebration).

## Competitor context
Differentiate from Wink (similar discovery app, larger but ad-heavy), HoYu (Snap-specific competitor), Yubo (younger demo). Vibe's lane is the **interest-based Snap discovery — not random matching**.

## Don't-do list
- **Strict on age safety** — the audience skews young. Don't run paid ads in dating-app contexts.
- Avoid dating-app-style framings. Vibe is FRIENDSHIP, not romance — keep that bright line clean.
- Don't show real users' Snap usernames in ads.
- Avoid framings that feel predatory or that age-down.
