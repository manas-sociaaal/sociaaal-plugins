# BabyLab — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
AI baby generator. Two faces in → one hypothetical baby out. Photo-AI category. One of Sociaaal's flagship apps with massive viral potential — celebrity pairings drive millions of organic views per cycle.

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Couples (esp. early-relationship Gen Z, 18–28), parents-to-be, family-curious women.
- **Secondary:** Celebrity-obsessed fans, identity-curious teens, friend groups doing for-fun pairings.
- **Where they live:** TikTok #babylab / #aibaby / #couplesoftiktok, Instagram Reels couples-content, YouTube Shorts celeb-pairing videos. Comment sections of relationship creators.
- **Motivation:** Curiosity ("what would our kid look like?"), parasocial fantasy (celebrity baby pairings), social currency (post-able novelty).

## Core promise
**See your future child — or any pairing's baby — in seconds.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Cute, share-able output that begs for a comment-bait pairing
2. Speed — under a minute from upload to result
3. Surprise factor — the AI baby often looks shockingly plausible
4. Conversation starter for couples

## Past creative observations
- P0_X batches in `NEW PHASE GATE/BABYLAB/` show heavy rotation of: comment-bait celeb pairings, "what would OUR baby look like" couple POVs, and reaction-format reveals.
- Observed hooks include "comment a couple", "Zendaya × Tom Holland", "what if [celeb pair] had a baby".
- Formats burned: face-merge → static reveal with caption.
- Lane mostly OPEN: side-by-side dual-baby comparison (one celeb pair vs another), age-progression of the AI baby, sibling-style group reveals.

## Cultural moments to ride
- Currently-trending celebrity couples (movies/music tour cycles drop new pairings constantly)
- Royalty / heir announcements
- Reality TV finales (couples reveals, engagement specials)
- Specific celebrity baby announcements (use as comparison: "AI predicted vs reality")

## Format playbook
**Works:** face-merge reveal, before/after, split-screen pairings, photo-to-image with motion, comment-bait POVs.
**Doesn't:** long talking head, complex multi-person live action, scripted dialogue.
**AI-asset fit:** Strong. NanoBanana handles the baby reveal still; Kling adds subtle parallax / pulse zoom for impact.

## Competitor context
Differentiate from Remini's age-progression, FaceApp's age filters, Reface's face-swap. BabyLab's lane is **two-parent baby generation**, which most competitors don't do well.

## Don't-do list
- Never depict real celebrities' actual babies (use only the AI-generated output).
- Never imply factual accuracy ("this IS what their baby looks like") — always frame as fun/AI prediction.
- Avoid pairings of minors. Couples must read as 18+.
- Avoid borderline-creepy "your future baby with a stranger" framings.

## Open research questions
- Which celebrity pairings drive the highest installs (vs just views)? Need attribution data.
- Does "predict our baby" outperform "celeb pair baby" for installs?
- What's the saturation curve — when does a celeb pairing trend stop converting?
