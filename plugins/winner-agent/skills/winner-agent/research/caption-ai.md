# Caption AI — Research Dossier

## App at a glance
AI caption generator for social posts. Describe the photo or paste it, get a caption. Text-AI category. Medium AI-fit — generation screen recordings + AI photo backdrops.

## Audience profile
- **Primary:** Social-media-active users (16–35), aspiring content creators, small-business owners managing their own social.
- **Secondary:** Marketers writing brand captions, students captioning Instagram aesthetic posts.
- **Where they live:** TikTok #captionhack / #instagramcaptions / #socialmediatips, Twitter/X marketing accounts, LinkedIn solo-marketer threads.
- **Motivation:** Never struggle for a caption. Match the caption energy to the photo. Sound clever.

## Core promise
**Never struggle for a caption again — AI matches the caption to the photo.**

## Value props (ranked)
1. Photo-aware (analyzes image content for relevant captions)
2. Tone variety — sincere, funny, aesthetic, hashtag-heavy, minimal
3. Speed — 5 seconds vs 5 minutes of "what do I write?"
4. Hashtag suggestions included

- "Caption of the day" creator series (social-marketer trend)

- Brand / influencer monetization content cycles

## Format playbook
**Works:** before/after caption reveal, screen recording of generation, talking-head "use this hack" content.
**Doesn't:** live-action heavy / non-screen content.
**AI-asset fit:** Medium. Generation screen recordings + AI-stylized backdrops (aesthetic photo placeholders).

## Competitor context
Differentiate from generic ChatGPT (no photo awareness), Buffer / Hootsuite (enterprise tools), Plann (planning-focused). Caption AI's lane is the **photo-aware, fast, mobile-first caption tool with tone variety**.

## Don't-do list
- Don't generate captions that fabricate facts ("this is the best restaurant in X" when user just took a photo).
- Avoid hashtag spam outputs — keep it tasteful.
- Don't train on copyrighted captioned content without licensing.
